Who can see what inside the app

The database itself enforces these rules, not just the screens.

  • Other staff never see your pay, hours, tax or personal contact details. Colleagues can see a work email only if the employer shares work emails.
  • Your employer (owner and HR) sees the shifts, pay and records for their business, from the date you chose to share. They never see your tax code, student loan or pension settings, or jobs you hold elsewhere.
  • Managers see their own team's shifts and rotas. They see more only if the owner or HR gives them a permission, such as approving hours, holidays or expenses for their team. Managers never see staff records or personal contact details unless the owner or HR gives access.
  • Your personal phone and emergency contact are seen only if you choose to share them, and only by the people the owner or HR grants access to. You can see who has access.
  • Your calendar shows only your own shifts and holidays, never anyone else's. Copies of shifts cancelled by a closed day are seen only by the owner and HR.
  • Online store money and orders are seen only by the business owner. Managers allowed to manage stock see stock levels and can record refunds.

Receipt scanning

When you add a photo or PDF of a receipt, the app reads it on your own phone. The picture and its text aren't sent to us or anyone else to be read. What's kept is the receipt file you attached (as before) and the details you choose to save with it: the shop's name, the VAT amount, the shop's VAT number and the VAT rates. You can change any of them before saving.

The "HMRC check" shown with a receipt is worked out from HMRC's published rules. It's a guide for you to check, not tax advice.

Location, photos and contact details

These are off unless switched on, and only collected when needed.

  • Location at clock-in: only if your employer turns on the location check for your site. Your phone's location is read once when you clock in or out, to check you're at work. We don't track you between clock-ins. Some staff can be marked as not needing it.
  • Photo at a shared clock-in screen: only if your employer turns it on, to show who clocked in. Photos are deleted automatically after 30 days, and all of them straight away if the employer turns the check off.
  • Personal phone, email and emergency contact: only if you add them and agree to share them. You can stop sharing at any time.

Sending tax updates to HMRC (Making Tax Digital)

If you're self-employed you can choose to send your quarterly updates and year-end figures to HMRC from the app.

  • You sign in on HMRC's own website and allow Wage Tracker to send updates for you. We never see your HMRC password. HMRC gives us a permission key, which we keep encrypted on our server so you don't have to sign in every quarter. You can cancel it at any time (Disconnect in the app, or in your HMRC account).
  • Your National Insurance number is stored encrypted and used only when sending to HMRC. Nobody else using Wage Tracker can see it, including any employer.
  • Nothing is sent until you've checked the figures and pressed Send.
  • Fraud prevention information. By law, HMRC requires software to send information about the device and connection with every request, to help stop fraud. For you this is: a random ID for your device, your browser's description, your screen and window sizes, your time zone, your IP address, your Wage Tracker account ID, the app's version, and when you last entered a two-step sign-in code (with a scrambled reference to the device). We send it only to HMRC, only when you use these features, and don't use it for anything else.
  • HMRC is responsible for what it does with information it receives, under its own privacy notice.

Two-step sign-in

Business owners, HR, managers and anyone sending to HMRC must use a code from an authenticator app as well as their password. Anyone else can turn it on. If you lose your phone you can use a backup code, or ask for an email link: after you click it there's a 24-hour wait and we email a warning with a cancel link. When two-step sign-in is reset we sign out your other devices and tell you by email and in the app.

Security, children, storage on your device, changes

  • Security: data is encrypted when sent and when stored. Access rules in the database stop people seeing data that isn't theirs. Passwords are never stored in readable form. Accounts that can see other people's details, or send to HMRC, need two-step sign-in, and the database refuses their requests until the code is entered.
  • Children: Wage Tracker is for people aged 16 and over. Where someone under 18 works, the employer is responsible for their records.
  • Storage on your device: if you tick "Remember me" when signing in, the app keeps your sign-in and a copy of your data on that device so it opens quickly and works offline. If you don't, they're kept only until you close the app. Signing out always removes them. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
