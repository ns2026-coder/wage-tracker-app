Who can see what inside the app

The database itself enforces these rules, not just the screens.

  • Other staff never see your pay, hours, tax or personal contact details. Colleagues can see a work email only if the employer shares work emails.
  • Your employer (owner and HR) sees the shifts, pay and records for their business, from the date you chose to share. They never see your tax code, student loan or pension settings, or jobs you hold elsewhere.
  • Managers see their own team's shifts and rotas. They see more only if the owner or HR gives them a permission, such as approving hours, holidays or expenses for their team. Managers never see staff records or personal contact details unless the owner or HR gives access.
  • Your personal phone and emergency contact are seen only if you choose to share them, and only by the people the owner or HR grants access to. You can see who has access.

Location, photos and contact details

These are off unless switched on, and only collected when needed.

  • Location at clock-in: only if your employer turns on the location check for your site. Your phone's location is read once when you clock in or out, to check you're at work. We don't track you between clock-ins. Some staff can be marked as not needing it.
  • Photo at a shared clock-in screen: only if your employer turns it on, to show who clocked in. Photos are deleted automatically after 30 days, and all of them straight away if the employer turns the check off.
  • Personal phone, email and emergency contact: only if you add them and agree to share them. You can stop sharing at any time.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the data we hold about you and get a copy
  • correct anything wrong
  • have your data deleted, unless we must keep it by law
  • limit or object to how we use it
  • take your data with you in a common format (you can export your shifts in the app)
  • withdraw consent at any time, e.g. stop sharing your contact details

To use any of these, email [privacy email]. It's free and we'll answer within one month. If the data is held for your employer, we'll pass your request to them and help them answer.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113. We'd like the chance to sort it out first.

Security, children, storage on your device, changes

  • Security: data is encrypted when sent and when stored. Access rules in the database stop people seeing data that isn't theirs. Passwords are never stored in readable form.
  • Children: Wage Tracker is for people aged 16 and over. Where someone under 18 works, the employer is responsible for their records.
  • Storage on your device: if you tick "Remember me" when signing in, the app keeps your sign-in and a copy of your data on that device so it opens quickly and works offline. If you don't, they're kept only until you close the app. Signing out always removes them. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Changes: if we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
